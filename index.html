DriveCast
A simple web browser built for discovering and playing web video. Add DriveCast to your iPhone Home Screen for an app-like experience.

Quick Start

About DriveCast

DriveCast can open websites and discover web video.

Some services such as YouTube and Vimeo prevent their pages from being displayed inside embedded browsers. DriveCast therefore opens those services directly.

AirPlay availability depends on iOS, the website and the video player.
